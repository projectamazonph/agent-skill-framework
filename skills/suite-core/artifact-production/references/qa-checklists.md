# QA Checklists

## Content

- Required sections are complete.
- Facts, calculations, names, dates, and units are verified.
- Corrections and exclusions are preserved.
- No placeholders, duplicated sections, unsupported claims, or fake sources remain.

## Visual

- Hierarchy, typography, color, spacing, and alignment are consistent.
- No clipping, overflow, awkward page break, unreadable density, or low contrast remains.
- Charts and visuals are accurate, labeled, and necessary.

## Functional

- File opens in the target application.
- Links, navigation, formulas, filters, controls, media, and exports work.
- Responsive, print, page, slide, or sheet views were inspected as applicable.

## Accessibility

- Heading order, reading order, alt text, table headers, labels, and contrast are acceptable.
- Meaning does not depend only on color or position.

## Identity and Delivery

- Filename, version, status, owner, and date are correct.
- Canonical and exported formats match.
- The final file contains no temporary assets or hidden review notes.
- Existing artifact identity and version history is preserved for updates.
