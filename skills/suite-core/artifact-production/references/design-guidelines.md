# Design Guidelines

## Hierarchy

- Use size, weight, contrast, and spacing to show importance.
- Limit simultaneous visual priorities.
- Keep repeated components structurally consistent.

## Typography

- Use at most two font families unless a brand system requires more.
- Use readable body sizes and line lengths.
- Avoid all-caps paragraphs, excessive bolding, and decorative fonts for body copy.

## Color

- Use brand colors when supplied.
- Keep semantic colors consistent for success, warning, risk, and neutral information.
- Maintain readable contrast and provide text or icon reinforcement.

## Layout

- Use a stable spacing scale and alignment grid.
- Prefer whitespace over decorative clutter.
- Keep related information close and unrelated sections distinct.
- Design for the target page, screen, or mobile viewport from the start.

## Visuals

- Use charts for real numeric relationships and diagrams for real structure.
- Avoid generic stock imagery, decorative data, and AI-looking filler.
- Label sources and dates near the visual they support.

## Accessibility

- Use meaningful headings, alt text, link labels, table headers, and reading order.
- Do not rely on color alone.
- Preserve keyboard usability for interactive artifacts.
- Avoid flashing, tiny targets, and low-contrast secondary text.
