# Artifact Standards

## Documents and PDFs

- Use one heading hierarchy and generated navigation for long documents.
- Keep margins, headers, footers, lists, tables, captions, and page breaks consistent.
- Avoid orphan headings, clipped tables, stranded captions, and excessive blank pages.
- Use selectable text and meaningful link labels.
- Render every page before delivery.

## Presentations

- Use 16:9 unless another format is required.
- Keep one primary message per slide.
- Use short headlines, meaningful visuals, and deliberate whitespace.
- Avoid dense paragraphs and tiny tables.
- Keep layout, title placement, page numbers, and source treatment consistent.
- Verify in slideshow and rendered-page views.

## Spreadsheets

- Separate inputs, raw data, calculations, outputs, and instructions when practical.
- Use explicit units, number formats, frozen headers, filters, validation, and protected formula areas.
- Keep formulas auditable and avoid unexplained hard-coded values.
- Label charts, axes, dates, currencies, and comparison periods.
- Recalculate and inspect formulas, errors, blank states, and print areas.

## Dashboards

- Lead with decisions and exceptions, not vanity metrics.
- Define KPI formulas, time windows, filters, and source freshness.
- Include empty, loading, error, and mobile states.
- Keep color meaning consistent and never rely on color alone.

## SOPs and Operational Guides

Include purpose, trigger, scope, owner, inputs, tools, prerequisites, numbered steps, decision points, exceptions, QA, outputs, evidence, escalation, and version owner.

## Briefs and Reports

Separate facts, analysis, decisions, risks, and next actions. Trace claims to sources and label inference.

## Audio and Video Scripts

Include timing, scene or beat, spoken copy, on-screen text, visual direction, transition, pronunciation, and asset notes. Keep spoken language natural.

## Repository Records and Templates

Store canonical title, description, owner, source project, tags, status, version, format, sensitivity, and update date. Keep templates content-neutral and validation-ready.
