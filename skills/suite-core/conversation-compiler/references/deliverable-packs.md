# Deliverable Packs

## Coding Project Pack

Order:

1. Project brief and problem definition
2. PRD and acceptance criteria
3. MVP scope and exclusions
4. Architecture and data model
5. API, state, security, and permission contracts
6. Build plan and atomic task list
7. Test, evaluation, and release plan
8. Coding-agent prompts
9. Deployment and operations documentation

## Course Creation Pack

Order:

1. Audience and competency outcomes
2. Curriculum map and prerequisite path
3. Module and lesson specifications
4. Teaching guides and scripts
5. Student handouts and worksheets
6. Practice drills and simulators
7. Assessments and rubrics
8. Presentations and downloadable materials
9. Delivery, feedback, and update plan

## Company Documentation Pack

Order:

1. Operating model and scope
2. Roles, ownership, and RACI
3. Policies and decision rules
4. SOPs and exception paths
5. Checklists and templates
6. Trackers and dashboards
7. Training and onboarding materials
8. Reporting and review cadence
9. Versioning and governance

## Completeness Test

A pack is complete when:

- Every requested outcome maps to at least one deliverable.
- Every deliverable has source evidence and acceptance criteria.
- Dependencies are explicit.
- Rejected and deferred ideas remain separate from approved scope.
- No blocked deliverable contains fabricated content.
- The export format and responsible production skill are named.
