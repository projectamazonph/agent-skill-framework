# Council Output Contract

1. Executive verdict
2. Scope and materials reviewed
3. Council roster and why each member was selected
4. Confirmed strengths
5. Debate record with material disagreements and resolutions
6. Prioritized gap register
7. Enhanced implementation plan
8. Test and validation plan
9. Security, data, migration, operations, and rollback review
10. GO, CONDITIONAL GO, or NO-GO verdict
11. Ordered next actions with owners and evidence

## Evidence Labels

- Confirmed: directly supported by reviewed materials or executed checks
- Inferred: reasonable conclusion from evidence, explicitly labeled
- Missing: required evidence was unavailable
- Disputed: council could not resolve the tradeoff without a decision

Keep the debate concise enough to act on. Preserve the strongest counterargument even when the council reaches consensus.
